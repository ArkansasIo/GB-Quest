# Expansions

20 expansions, from `src/data_world.js`. Expansions 1-5 sit on the VALENDRIA map and are reached by ship from Ardenhold; 6-20 are worlds of their own, each with a painted map, reached through the portal (open from 5 levels below the expansion's range). Every expansion has three regions (8 zones), a town at its first region, dungeons, a raid, a 4-step campaign (EXPANSIONS in the menu), a story trailer, an emblem and its own theme, battle and boss music.

Expansions 14-20 were added in expansion wave 4 (`v: 4`). Their bosses come from their own seed after every earlier boss, so existing boss ids, names and saves are unchanged.

## The new expansions

### 14. CLOCKWORK EMPIRE: THE GEARS NEVER STOP

A mechanical empire wakes, and its brass armies begin to march. Infiltrate the Clockwork Empire and stop the Grand Engine before it winds the world down.

- **Levels:** 80-200
- **Regions:** BRASSGATE (badlands, 80-120); COGWORK CANYON (volcanic, 120-160); FOUNDRY SPIRES (crystal, 160-200)
- **Dungeons:** BRASSGATE DEPTHS (L110, lava); COGWORK CANYON DEPTHS (L140, lava); GEARHEART ARCHIVE (L155, void); FOUNDRY SPIRES UNDERCROFT (L170, lava)
- **Raid:** THE GRAND ENGINE (L190, 5 bosses)
- **Campaign:** Reach BRASSGATE → Defeat 12 foes across CLOCKWORK EMPIRE → Clear COGWORK CANYON DEPTHS → Defeat THE GRAND ENGINE. Reward 3600 XP and 5000 G.

### 15. TIDES OF THE LEVIATHAN: THE DEEP REMEMBERS

A leviathan older than the moon surfaces, and the tides obey only it. Sail the storm, gather the drowned clans, and descend into the leviathan itself.

- **Levels:** 140-280
- **Regions:** STORMWRACK SHORE (coast, 140-187); KELPDROWN FEN (marsh, 187-233); CORALWOOD (jungle, 233-280)
- **Dungeons:** STORMWRACK SHORE DEPTHS (L170, temple); KELPDROWN FEN DEPTHS (L200, temple); TIDECALLER SHRINE (L215, temple); CORALWOOD UNDERCROFT (L230, temple)
- **Raid:** THE LEVIATHAN'S GULLET (L270, 5 bosses)
- **Campaign:** Reach STORMWRACK SHORE → Defeat 12 foes across TIDES OF THE LEVIATHAN → Clear KELPDROWN FEN DEPTHS → Defeat THE LEVIATHAN'S GULLET. Reward 5040 XP and 7000 G.

### 16. SANDS OF ETERNITY: TIME IS A DESERT

A pharaoh who stole time rewinds the desert, unmaking everything that came after. Cross the endless dunes and break the hourglass before your own past is undone.

- **Levels:** 200-360
- **Regions:** DUNESEA (badlands, 200-253); MIRAGE COAST (coast, 253-307); SUNFORGE WASTES (volcanic, 307-360)
- **Dungeons:** DUNESEA DEPTHS (L230, crypt); MIRAGE COAST DEPTHS (L260, crypt); ORACLE'S SANDCOURT (L275, temple); SUNFORGE WASTES UNDERCROFT (L290, crypt)
- **Raid:** THE HOURGLASS TOMB (L350, 5 bosses)
- **Campaign:** Reach DUNESEA → Defeat 12 foes across SANDS OF ETERNITY → Clear MIRAGE COAST DEPTHS → Defeat THE HOURGLASS TOMB. Reward 6480 XP and 9000 G.

### 17. THE HOLLOW STARS: THE SKY IS EMPTYING

Something beyond the sky is eating the stars, and the falling husks bring horrors with them. Follow the fallen stars to the vault where the light is being stolen.

- **Levels:** 280-440
- **Regions:** STARFALL CRATER (crystal, 280-333); UMBRAL STEPPE (dusk, 333-387); HOARFROST REACH (snow, 387-440)
- **Dungeons:** STARFALL CRATER DEPTHS (L310, void); UMBRAL STEPPE DEPTHS (L340, void); ASTRAL ORRERY (L355, void); HOARFROST REACH UNDERCROFT (L370, void)
- **Raid:** THE STARLESS VAULT (L430, 5 bosses)
- **Campaign:** Reach STARFALL CRATER → Defeat 12 foes across THE HOLLOW STARS → Clear UMBRAL STEPPE DEPTHS → Defeat THE STARLESS VAULT. Reward 7920 XP and 11000 G.

### 18. COURTS OF THE FEY: EVERY BARGAIN HAS A PRICE

The Summer and Winter courts break their ancient truce and spill into the mortal world. Bargain with tricksters and queens, and end the fey war before it ends you.

- **Levels:** 360-520
- **Regions:** MOONPETAL WILDS (jungle, 360-413); GLAMOUR GLADE (crystal, 413-467); EVERSPRING (meadow, 467-520)
- **Dungeons:** MOONPETAL WILDS DEPTHS (L390, temple); GLAMOUR GLADE DEPTHS (L420, temple); HALL OF MASKS (L435, void); EVERSPRING UNDERCROFT (L450, temple)
- **Raid:** THE THORNED THRONE (L510, 5 bosses)
- **Campaign:** Reach MOONPETAL WILDS → Defeat 12 foes across COURTS OF THE FEY → Clear GLAMOUR GLADE DEPTHS → Defeat THE THORNED THRONE. Reward 9360 XP and 13000 G.

### 19. IRON LEGION: THE DRUMS OF WAR

The Iron Legion, an army that cannot die, marches south under a general who has never lost. Rally the free kingdoms, break the siege lines and storm the Iron Citadel.

- **Levels:** 460-640
- **Regions:** WARFROST PLAINS (snow, 460-520); LEGION SCAR (badlands, 520-580); ANVIL MOUNTAINS (volcanic, 580-640)
- **Dungeons:** WARFROST PLAINS DEPTHS (L490, ice); LEGION SCAR DEPTHS (L520, ice); LEGION WAR FORGE (L535, lava); ANVIL MOUNTAINS UNDERCROFT (L550, ice)
- **Raid:** THE IRON CITADEL (L630, 5 bosses)
- **Campaign:** Reach WARFROST PLAINS → Defeat 12 foes across IRON LEGION → Clear LEGION SCAR DEPTHS → Defeat THE IRON CITADEL. Reward 11520 XP and 16000 G.

### 20. TWILIGHT OF THE GODS: THE LAST DAWN

The old gods return, not to save the world, but to end it and begin again. Stand at the end of all things and decide whether the world deserves a new dawn.

- **Levels:** 620-720
- **Regions:** ASHEN OLYMPUS (dusk, 620-653); GODFALL RIFT (volcanic, 653-687); LAST DAWN (crystal, 687-720)
- **Dungeons:** ASHEN OLYMPUS DEPTHS (L650, void); GODFALL RIFT DEPTHS (L680, void); PANTHEON RUINS (L695, temple); LAST DAWN UNDERCROFT (L710, void)
- **Raid:** THE FALL OF HEAVEN (L710, 5 bosses)
- **Campaign:** Reach ASHEN OLYMPUS → Defeat 12 foes across TWILIGHT OF THE GODS → Clear GODFALL RIFT DEPTHS → Defeat THE FALL OF HEAVEN. Reward 12960 XP and 18000 G.
