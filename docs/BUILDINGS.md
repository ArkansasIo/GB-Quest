# Hub buildings

Every wild region hub is a settlement with buildings you can use. Face any tile of a building and press A, or walk into its door from below.

| Building | What it does | Where |
|---|---|---|
| INN | Long rest, set your hearth, hire and manage companions | Villages and towns |
| GENERAL STORE | Buy and sell | Villages and towns |
| SHRINE | PRAY: free HP and MP for the party and BLESSED for 3 fights, once every 10 minutes | Villages and towns |
| WINDMILL | BUY BREAD: WELL FED for 5 fights | Villages and towns, where there is room |
| SMITHY | Repair, upgrade and craft gear | Towns |
| STABLE | Mounts | Towns |
| POST OFFICE | Mail | Towns |
| BANK | Your stash | Towns |
| HOUSES and COTTAGES | Talk to the townsfolk | Villages and towns |

Towns are every third region and the first region of each expansion; the other hubs are villages. There are 17 towns and 33 villages, with 381 buildings in all. Elderwood (hand-made) and the city of Ardenhold keep their own layouts.

Buildings are placed by src/gen.js (placeHubBuildings) from their own seed, after everything else on the map, so no node, boss, exit or entrance moves. A building never covers any of those and is skipped if it would cut anything off from the hub. Shops get a lane from the door to the camp. src/buildings.js draws them and opens them.
