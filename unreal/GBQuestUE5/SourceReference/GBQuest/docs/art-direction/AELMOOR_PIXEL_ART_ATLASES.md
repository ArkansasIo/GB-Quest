# Aelmoor pixel-art atlas additions

## Biome terrain atlas
`assets/mmorpg/lanterns-of-aelmoor/biome-tiles-16.svg` contains eight tile families on a 16px grid: forest, grassland, desert, tundra, volcanic, swamp, coast and crystal. It is source art, not a baked screenshot. Keep nearest-neighbour sampling and integer scaling when importing into the 160×144 handheld viewport.

## Monster and spell-effect atlas
`assets/mmorpg/lanterns-of-aelmoor/monsters-and-spell-fx.svg` contains six original monster silhouettes (Moss Wolf, Ember Imp, Frost Wisp, Bog Crawler, Crystal Golem and Shadow Knight), six elemental spell glyphs, and a modular mountain/rock strip. Use the 32px character cells as concept placeholders until directional animation frames are generated.

## Palette
The atlas family uses the expansion palette: midnight navy, slate, moss, amber, cream, ember and violet. The art is original and does not reproduce third-party game sprites.
