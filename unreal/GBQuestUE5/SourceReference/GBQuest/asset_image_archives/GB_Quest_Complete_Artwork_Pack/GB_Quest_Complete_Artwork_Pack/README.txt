CONTENTS
This archive contains seven generated full-colour fantasy pixel-art overview sheets covering:
1. Playable characters, NPCs, and animation concepts
2. Enemies, creatures, and bosses
3. Weapons, armor, and equipment
4. Items, consumables, buildings, and town props
5. World tiles, biomes, dungeons, and environments
6. UI, menus, HUD, icons, and game-system mockups
7. HD64-style portraits, story/cutscene art, maps, banners, and promotional artwork

IMPORTANT PRODUCTION NOTE
These PNGs are composite concept/catalog sheets. They are not yet individual transparent sprite exports, animation strips, or engine-ready tilesets. Some lettering and tiny details in generated artwork may be illustrative rather than production-accurate. Before integrating into GB Quest, the artwork must be reviewed, sliced into individual assets, assigned dimensions/origins, and mapped to the repository's existing asset IDs and manifests.

The original game asset inventory is not included in this archive. No repository files have been modified by creating this ZIP.
